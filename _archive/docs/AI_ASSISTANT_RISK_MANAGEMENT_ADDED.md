### **Multi-Pair Guidelines**
- Maximum simultaneous positions: 3-5
- Total portfolio risk: 5-10% maximum
- High correlation pairs list
- Independent pairs list
- Correlation management rules

### **Account Size Recommendations**
- **Micro** ($100-$500): 0.01-0.03 lots, 2-5% risk, 2-3 positions
- **Small** ($500-$2,000): 0.03-0.10 lots, 2-3% risk, 3-5 positions
- **Standard** ($2,000-$10,000): 0.10-0.50 lots, 1-2% risk, 5-7 positions
- **Large** ($10,000+): 0.50-2.00 lots, 0.5-1% risk, 7-10 positions

### **Daily Limits**
- **Conservative**: 2% max loss, 5-10 trades, 2-3 consecutive losses
- **Moderate**: 3-5% max loss, 10-15 trades, 3-4 consecutive losses
- **Aggressive**: 5-7% max loss, 15-20 trades, 4-5 consecutive losses

Multi-Pair Portfolio Risk Management:

• Maximum simultaneous positions: 3-5 simultaneous positions
• Total portfolio risk: 5-10% maximum

  - EUR/USD & GBP/USD (typically move together)
  - AUD/USD & NZD/USD (commodity currencies)
  - EUR/USD & USD/CHF (inverse correlation)

### **User:** "What are the critical risk warnings?"
**AI Response:**
```
⚠️ CRITICAL RISK WARNINGS:

• NEVER risk more than 2% per single trade
• ALWAYS use stop losses on every position
• ALWAYS monitor correlation between open positions
• NEVER have more than 10% total portfolio risk
• NEVER trade during major news without wider stops
• NEVER overtrade after consecutive losses

Emergency Procedures:

If Account Drops 10%:
1. Close all positions immediately
2. Stop automated trading
3. Review all trades for errors
4. Reduce position size by 50% when resuming
5. Trade only highest-probability setups

If Consecutive Losses Exceed Threshold:
1. Pause trading for 24 hours
2. Review system settings
3. Check for changing market conditions
4. Consider reducing position size
5. Resume with 1 test trade
```
