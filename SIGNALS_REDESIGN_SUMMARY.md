## 🎯 Signal Types

### 1. BUY Signal (Green)
- Shows entry, stop loss, take profit
- "Execute BUY Signal" button
- Confidence meter
- All analysis scores

### 2. SELL Signal (Red)
- Shows entry, stop loss, take profit
- "Execute SELL Signal" button
- Confidence meter
- All analysis scores

### 3. NO_SIGNAL (Gray)
- Shows reason: "Market consolidating"
- "Set Alert" button only
- Lower confidence score
- Market context

### 4. CHOPPY (Orange)
- Warning about volatile market
- "Wait for clearer signal"
- Market sentiment info
